## Palette

Pine on warm paper, already in `replica/design/tokens.json`. Accent `#0c4f3e` on `#f6f3ec` is 8.60:1. Contrast script: 0 AA failures on 2026-10-10. No reference hex was sampled from a screenshot, so `brand.json` colors is empty rather than a guess.

## Logo brief

A plus cut into a rounded square, pine `#0c4f3e` on paper `#f6f3ec`. Wordmark set in Source Sans 3. Must read at 16px and as a 1024 opaque app icon. Do not use a wordmark that stacks two capital letters the way a competitor might, and do not use teal. SVG is `app/icon.svg`.

## Voice

Plain, calm, specific. Plain does not mean terse to the point of rudeness. Calm does not mean vague. Specific does not mean clinical jargon.

Do: say what the button does. Don't: promise HIPAA as a badge.
Do: "No sessions today." Don't: "Your schedule is empty — let's get started!"
Do: "Signed notes cannot be edited." Don't: "Oops, that note is locked."
Do: name the dollar amount. Don't: say "affordable" or "cheapest."
Do: tell them the agreement was not reviewed by counsel. Don't: imply a lawyer signed it.

Strings in the product: "Create your practice", "No sessions today.", "Add a client", "Book session", "Sign note", "Record cash", "Download superbill", "Intake received.", "This link has expired. Ask your clinician for a new one.", "Cancellation requested."
